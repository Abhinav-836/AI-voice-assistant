AI Voice Assistant

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

The AI Voice Assistant is a free demo built by Abhinav Ashutosh (Kavorin Tech). There are no accounts. This page explains what happens when you talk to it. We don't sell data or use it for advertising.

What happens to your voice

  • Audio: while a session is running, your microphone audio is streamed through LiveKit to our agent. Deepgram turns it into text. We don't record or store the audio.
  • Answers: the text of what you say goes to an AI model from OpenAI or Groq, which writes the reply, and Rime turns the reply into speech.
  • Look-ups: when you ask about stocks, weather, news or facts, the topic of the question (for example a ticker or a city) is sent to those data services.

What we store

  • Transcripts: the text of each session (what you said and what the assistant replied), the tools it used, and your voice settings, so the session can be reviewed. Only someone with that session's private key, and the site's admin, can open it.
  • Server logs and limits: your IP address and session times, used to stop abuse and apply the daily session limit.

Don't say passwords, card numbers or other sensitive details to the assistant.

Who else handles it

  • LiveKit (audio streaming), Deepgram (speech to text), OpenAI and Groq (AI replies), Rime (text to speech).
  • Finnhub, Yahoo Finance, weather, news and encyclopedia services, for look-ups.
  • Render runs the server.

Deleting your data

To have a session's transcript deleted, email ashutoshabhinavbachra0009@gmail.com with the session time.
